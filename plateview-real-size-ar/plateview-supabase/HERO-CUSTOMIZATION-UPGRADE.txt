PlateCrop hero customization upgrade

What changed:
- Hero headline text can be customized from Dashboard > Settings.
- Choose the exact dish used in the hero 3D viewer.
- Hero visual can be switched between 3D dish, image, and video.
- Hero videos can be uploaded/replaced later and autoplay muted + loop on the guest menu.
- Image/video/3D settings are per restaurant.
- No new Supabase SQL is required: the extra hero settings are stored inside the existing restaurants.menu_theme JSON field.

Install:
Replace the files from the update ZIP in your current project, commit, and redeploy Vercel.
