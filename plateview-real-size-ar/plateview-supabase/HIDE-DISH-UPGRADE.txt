PlateCrop — hide individual dishes

A new "Visible on menu" toggle has been added to each dish.

- ON: the dish appears on the public guest menu.
- OFF: the dish is completely hidden from the public guest menu.
- The dish remains saved in the dashboard and can be re-enabled later.
- "Available" remains separate. This lets you keep a sold-out/unavailable dish visible without deleting it.
- Hidden dishes show a "Hidden" badge in the dashboard so you can identify them easily.

No Supabase SQL change is required because this uses the existing menu_items.published field.
