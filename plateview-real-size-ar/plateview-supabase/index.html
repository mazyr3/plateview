QUESTIONS

Before you put it on the table.

Do guests need an app?

No. They scan the table QR code and use the menu directly in the browser.

Can restaurant owners edit their own menu?

Yes. Each owner gets a private dashboard for dishes, translations, QR codes, branding and analytics.

What happens if a subscription stops?

The dashboard becomes locked while the restaurant data stays stored, so access can be restored after renewal.

Can I change my menu whenever I want?

Yes. Prices, availability, categories, descriptions, models and branding can all be updated from the dashboard.
