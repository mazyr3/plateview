PlateCrop — independent dish visuals

New per-dish controls:
1. Menu card visual: Automatic / Photo / 3D model
2. Opened dish visual: Automatic / Photo / 3D model

These settings are independent. A dish can use:
- Photo on the menu card + 3D after opening
- 3D on the menu card + Photo after opening
- Photo in both places
- 3D in both places

If only a photo exists, the opened dish now shows that photo instead of an empty 3D area.
If the chosen asset is unavailable, PlateCrop falls back to the other available asset.
AR remains available whenever the dish has a 3D model, even when the opened dish is displaying its photo.

No Supabase SQL change is needed. The two preferences are stored in translations._meta.
